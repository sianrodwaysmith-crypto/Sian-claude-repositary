Morning Briefing — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This is a personal app built for private use by a single individual.

Data we access

  • Whoop: We access your personal recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate and sleep data solely to display it to you within the app.
  • Apple Calendar: We access your personal calendar events solely to display them to you within the app.
  • Location: We access your location solely to fetch local weather data.

How we handle your data

  • No data is collected or stored on any server.
  • No data is shared with any third parties.
  • All data remains locally on your device in your browser storage only.
  • This app has no other users — it is a private personal tool.
